How thresholds are set (fixed)
NormalBase risk locked at 1.00% per trade
CautionDrawdown ≥ 3% below start
WarningDrawdown ≥ 5% below start
StopDrawdown ≥ 7% below start
Risk halves at Caution, quarters at Warning, and stops at Stop — a losing streak of 3+ can trigger the same cuts even before drawdown catches up. A winning streak of 3+ can raise risk slightly, but only while still Normal. Drawdown here only counts equity below your starting balance — giving back open profit doesn't count.
